## Scope and mode

Dedicated `/sortie` marketing surface in **Persuade** mode, with supporting
registry, homepage, and `/ios` links.

## Audience, job, action, and proof

iPhone strategy players should understand Sortie's permanent
supply-line mechanic, believe the 1,500-level campaign is concrete, and leave
with an honest picture of its pre-release state. Current Godot footage, the
byte-identical app icon, current keep and biome art, executable level data,
local-save code, Game Center bridge, iPhone-only Xcode target, and public-store
lookup are the proof.

## Direction and memorable moment

A 15-biome campaign atlas threaded by one permanent supply line. The memorable
moment is watching the real pre-release capture while source-art keeps route
through the first viewport, then following that same line into the campaign.

## Constraints and unresolved decisions

No public App Store acquisition control, final price, or TestFlight claim.
Synthetic source-art compositions are labeled and never presented as
screenshots. Motion pauses on request and under reduced motion. Public release
timing and price remain unresolved.
