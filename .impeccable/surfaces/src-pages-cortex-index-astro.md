## Scope and mode

Dedicated `/cortex` marketing surface in **Persuade** mode, with supporting links
and real imagery on the homepage and `/ios`.

## Audience, job, action, and proof

iPhone players should understand Cortex as one self-contained native hub,
recognize the range across its eight games, and continue into the Nintek iOS story.
The hub capture and all eight game-home captures are the proof. The page must state
optional Game Center, no Nintek backend, and bundle `com.nintek.cortex` without
inventing multiplayer, economies, or sync.

Current default-branch evidence records a private pre-release draft and no
uploaded replacement build. Do not claim public TestFlight or App Store
availability, and do not guess the outcome of later distribution work.

## Direction and memorable moment

An arcade constellation in depth: the real hub anchors the first viewport, a sticky
eight-game index resolves each game into its own color field, and the actual phone
screen remains the center of every chapter. The memorable moment is scrolling from
the shared hub into eight distinct, truthful game worlds.

## Constraints

Inherit Nintek navigation and product-led craft while letting Cortex become the
portfolio's most vivid page. Responsive media must be optimized and stable, below-fold
screens lazy-loaded, all interactions keyboard-safe, and motion fully removed under
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
