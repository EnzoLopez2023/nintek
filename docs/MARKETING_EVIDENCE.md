## Publication rules

- A build, deployment, App Store Connect record, or TestFlight assignment is not
  evidence of public availability.
- Do not infer a price, territory, approval, submission, support channel,
  privacy route, hostname cutover, or release date.
- Product icons are copied byte-for-byte from immutable upstream commits. The
  site does not resize, re-encode, round, or otherwise derive those master files.
- Simulator captures, deterministic fixtures, source art, social composites,
  and physical-device evidence remain distinct classes.
- Nintek.com is downstream marketing copy. No fact in this repository drives a
  change in an app repository.

## Route and index policy

Indexed routes are `/`, `/about`, `/ios`, `/terms`, `/tare`, `/workshop`, and
`/cairn`.

Product-specific privacy and support routes remain public and canonical but
`noindex, follow`. Private or evidence-limited products (`/shopkeep`, `/tabloom`,
`/pulsewire`, `/hearth`, `/prism`, `/watchtower`, `/lantern`, `/marquee`, and
`/shapepilot`) are also `noindex, follow`.

No Prism, Watchtower, Lantern, Marquee, or ShapePilot privacy/support page is
created: their audited repositories and the release ledger publish no such
policy or support URL. Their overview pages link only to immutable product
evidence, shared Nintek terms, and the established studio contact.

## Refresh procedure

1. Read the product authority at an immutable commit.
2. Fetch the exact canonical 1024 px master and verify SHA-256 before placement.
3. Copy the bytes without image processing and verify SHA-256 again.
4. Update `canonicalIcons`, registry copy, route policy, and social sources
   together.
5. Run `npm run social:check` and `npm run check`; the site checker rejects a
   missing, extra, wrong-sized, stale, or byte-different top-level product icon.
